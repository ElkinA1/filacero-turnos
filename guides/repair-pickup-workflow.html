FilaCero · Service operations

Plan a repair pickup before calling the customer

At a repair counter, a finished work order does not always mean the item is ready for pickup. A technician may still need a test bench, accessories and the right handover record.

Walk one item to the counter

Use a dummy job number. Have a colleague locate the device, match its accessories and reserve space for a demonstration. Ask the front desk what evidence they need before calling the next ticket.

Measure a handover you can influence

Count the pickups that were ready when the visitor arrived and divide by the number you observed. Record the reason for each exception. Use a comparable shift before changing appointment capacity.

For a live walkthrough, bring a sample service type and the number of counters at one location. The interface shown is the Spanish-language demo.
